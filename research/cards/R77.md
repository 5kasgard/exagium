## Measured (2)
- **method**: A near‑duplicate detector can estimate whether articles share a common origin by identifying copy links. — "In 200,000 English articles from CC-News, a near-duplicate detector identifies copy links to another outlet for 18.9% of the articles, a detector-based lower bound." [paper: Copies Are Not Confirmations: How News Duplication Fools LLM Judges and Continual Learners, 2026; https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.09203]
- **result**: Discounting dependent reports by keeping only one article per detected copy chain reduces the acceptance of copied false claims. — "In a simulated continual-learning stream, showing the judge one report per detected copy chain cut the copied lies accepted by 0.10-0.24 across four judges, at a mean cost of at most 4.2 points of accepted truths." [paper: Copies Are Not Confirmations: How News Duplication Fools LLM Judges and Continual Learners, 2026; https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2611.09203]
