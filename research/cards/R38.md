## Reported (1)
- **method**: The Generic Holdout controls false discoveries by splitting data into an exploration set and a holdout set and limiting the information revealed from the holdout. — "Our solution, the Generic Holdout, involves two simple ingredients: (1) a partitioning of the data into a exploration set and a holdout set and (2) a limited exposure strategy for the holdout set."
