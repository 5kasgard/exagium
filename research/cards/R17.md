## Measured (3)
- **result**: GPT‑4 judges achieve over 80% agreement with human preferences, matching human‑human agreement. — "strong LLM judges like GPT-4 can match both controlled and crowdsourced human preferences well, achieving over 80% agreement, the same level of agreement between humans." [paper: Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena, 2023; https://doi.org/10.52202/075280-2020]
- **result**: Even the best LLM judges can differ from human scores by up to 5 points. — "they are still quite far behind inter-human agreement and their assigned scores may still differ with up to 5 points from human-assigned scores." [paper: Judging the Judges: Evaluating Alignment and Vulnerabilities in LLMs-as-Judges, 2024; https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2406.12624]
- **result**: Subject‑matter experts agree with LLM judges 68% of the time in dietetics and 64% in mental health. — "SMEs agreed with LLM judges 68% of the time in the dietetics domain and 64% in mental health when evaluating overall preference." [paper: Limitations of the LLM-as-a-Judge Approach for Evaluating LLM Outputs in Expert Knowledge Tasks, 2025; https://doi.org/10.1145/3708359.3712091]
