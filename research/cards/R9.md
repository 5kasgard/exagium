## Reported (3)
- **result**: Only about half of long‑context models can keep satisfactory performance at their advertised 32 K token window. — "While these models all claim context sizes of 32K tokens or greater, only half of them can maintain satisfactory performance at the length of 32K." [paper: RULER: What's the Real Context Size of Your Long-Context Language Models?, 2024; https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2404.06654]
- **failure mode**: Most LLMs fail to capture long‑range dependencies even when the entire document fits inside their advertised context window. — "most LLMs have shockingly bad long context ability and fail to capture long dependencies in the context, even when their context window size is enough to fit the entire document." [paper: LooGLE: Can Long-Context Language Models Understand Long Contexts?, 2024; https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.acl-long.859]
- **failure mode**: Higher overthinking scores—i.e., longer internal reasoning chains—correlate with lower task performance, especially for reasoning‑focused models. — "higher overthinking scores correlate with decreased performance, with reasoning models exhibiting stronger tendencies toward overthinking compared to non-reasoning models." [paper: The Danger of Overthinking: Examining the Reasoning-Action Dilemma in Agentic Tasks, 2025; https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-c-000793179]
