# Gemini report (2026-11-15), as received via the owner. Chat-only track; not edited.

Tested in `claude-2026-11-15.md`.

Static analysis of BRIEF.md and the reference/ disassembly shows that linear (~670 cycles/img measured at 3.8 GHz, estimate based on bin/clock) is bound by its 6-wide front-end allocation ceiling and a 5-way interleaved store-to-load forwarding accumulation chain on s[0..9], whereas mlp16 (~275 cycles/img measured at 3.8 GHz, estimate) runs well above its ~100–134 cycles/img port/issue floor (estimate) because Layer 2's serial FP32 addps chain (~49 cycles, estimate), unrolled argmax dependencies (~25–35 cycles, estimate), and 3.14 data-dependent branch mispredictions per image (~50–63 cycles, estimate) serialize execution after Layer 1.
1. Host Microarchitecture & Port Model
From host-lscpu.txt, the host CPU is Intel Family 6, Model 173 (0xAD), Stepping 1 with 48 KiB L1d, 64 KiB L1i, 2 MiB L2 per core, and AVX-512/AMX flags — an Intel Xeon 6 (Granite Rapids / Redwood Cove P-core) (sharing the 6-wide allocation, 12-port layout of Golden Cove / Redwood Cove):
 * Allocation / Retirement width: 6 fused uops/cycle (estimate; ~5–5.7 uops/cycle sustained ceiling, estimate).
 * Load ports: 3× load ports (P2, P3, P11; up to 3 loads/cycle from L1d, or ~2 loads/cycle under L1d bank conflicts, estimate).
 * Store ports: 2× store-address/data ports (P4/P9, P7/P8; 2 stores/cycle, estimate).
 * Vector / FP ALU ports: 3× ports (P0, P1, P5) for paddw, mulps, addps, pcmpgtw, pand. Shuffle/extract/convert ops (pextrw, shufps, unpckhps, cvtsi2ss) require P5 (plus P0/P1 for pextrw/cvtsi2ss second uops); addps and mulps have 4-cycle latency (estimate), and paddw has 1-cycle latency (estimate).
 * Scalar Integer ALU & Branch ports: 5× scalar ALU ports (P0, P1, P5, P6, P10) and 2× branch ports (P0, P6).

Detailed Breakdown of linear (linear.objdump.txt)
 * Per-image fread & bias init (12d5–134d): Calls fread(px, 64, 1, x) per image (131.3 Ir/img inside glibc plus 15 Ir/img call wrapper), then copies b[0..9] to stack s[0..9] (0x20(%rsp)..0x47(%rsp)) via 3 loads and 4 stores (12 Ir/img).
 * Pixel loop .L12 (1350–1393, 64 iterations/img): Loads 1 pixel byte (movzbl (%rdi),%eax), converts to float (pxor, cvtsi2ss, shufps $0xe0), and runs .L11 5 times per pixel (320 iterations/img) using 64-bit (vector(2) float) SSE operations: movq (%rax),%xmm0 (loads 2 weights), movq (%rdx),%xmm1 (loads 2 accumulators s[2k..2k+1] from stack), add $8,%rax, add $8,%rdx, mulps %xmm2,%xmm0, addps %xmm0,%xmm1, movlps %xmm1,-8(%rdx) (stores 2 accumulators back to stack), cmp %rax,%rcx, jne 1365.
 * Argmax .L15 (13a0–13b9, 9 iterations/img): Compiled branchlessly using ucomiss 0x20(%rsp,%rcx,4),%xmm0 and cmova %edx,%eax, with only the loop-counter branch jne 13a0.
Detailed Breakdown of mlp16 (mlp16.objdump.txt)
 * Pre-loop setup (13eb–1666): fread reads all 95,600 images in one bulk call, validates px <= 16 in a 16-byte SIMD loop (36 Ir/img, 4 loads/img), holds B1[0..1] in %xmm8/%xmm7, B2[0..2] in %xmm11/%xmm10/%xmm9, W2[0][0..2] in %xmm14/%xmm13/%xmm12, and pre-copies W2[1..15][0..2] (720 B) onto the stack (0x00(%rsp)..0x2c0(%rsp)) once before the image loop.
 * Layer 1 (1695–179d, 8 iterations/img, unrolled by 8 pixels): Each iteration executes 8 byte loads (movzbl), 7 offset adds (add $0x11..$0x77), 8 shifts (shl $0x5), 2 pointer adds, 16 memory-operand paddw -0x1100(%rax,%r..,1), %xmm1/%xmm0 instructions, and 1 fused cmp/jne (43 × 8 = 344 Ir/img, 192 loads/img, 128 vector adds/img).
 * ReLU (17a3–17b7, 6 Ir/img) — Factual correction to BRIEF.md H4: In mlp16.objdump.txt (17a3–17b7), GCC completely eliminated short h[H] and memcpy(h + 8 * k, &a, sizeof a). There are 0 stores to h[] and 0 scalar loads from h[]; the ReLU outputs stay in %xmm4 (h[0..7]) and %xmm3 (h[8..15]) and are extracted directly into %eax via pextrw $0..7, %xmm4/%xmm3, %eax. H4 (store-to-load forwarding on h[]) is already implemented by the compiler and is 100% ruled out.
 * Layer 2 (17bb–1b9e, 196.65 Ir/img): Unrolled across all 16 hidden units. Each unit executes pextrw, test %ax,%ax, and je (48 Ir/img). From mlp16.cachegrind-lines.txt (1,178,600 / 95,600), an average of 12.3285 of 16 hidden units (77.1%) are active per image (0.822 for j=0, 11.506 for j=1\dots 15). Each active unit j \ge 1 executes 12 instructions: cwtl, pxor, cvtsi2ss, shufps, 3× 16-byte stack loads of W2[j][0..2] (34.52 loads/img), 3× mulps, and 3× addps accumulating into %xmm1 (s0), %xmm0 (s1), and %xmm2 (s2).
 * Argmax (1ba1–1c95, 52.0 Ir/img): Stores %xmm1, %xmm0, %xmm2 to stack 0x2f0..0x310(%rsp) (3 stores/img), then because of #pragma GCC unroll 10, emits 7 conditional branches (jbe) for c = 2\dots 8 comparing unpacked register lanes against 0x2f0(%rsp,%rax,4) (8 scalar stack loads/img), plus seta for c=1 and cmova for c=9.

Why linear takes ~670 cycles/image (Estimate)
 * Front-end allocation width (~570–635 cycles/img, estimate): Even after macro-fusing 395 branches, linear issues ~3,420 fused uops per image (3,812 instructions). On a 6-wide core sustaining ~5.3–5.7 instructions/cycle, allocating those instructions requires ~600–670 cycles/img (estimate).
 * Loop-carried store-to-load forwarding chain on s[0..9] (~576 cycles/img, estimate): Inside .L11, gcc did not keep s[0..9] in XMM registers; every pixel reloads s[2k..2k+1] from stack (movq (%rdx),%xmm1), executes addps (4 cycles latency, estimate), and stores back (movlps %xmm1,-8(%rdx)), incurring a ~5-cycle store-to-load forwarding latency (estimate) on the next pixel (4 + 5 = 9\text{ cycles/pixel}, estimate). Because 5 independent class-pairs are interleaved across 64 pixels, the 5 chains run in parallel within the ROB over 64 \times 9 = \mathbf{576\text{ cycles/img}} (estimate), plus ~35–45 cycles (estimate) for fread and argmax, totaling ~615–670 cycles/img (estimate).
Why mlp16 takes ~275 cycles/image (Estimate)
mlp16's pure port/allocation floor is only ~100–134 cycles/img (estimate), leaving ~145–175 cycles/img (estimate) unexplained by port throughput. Tracing the serial dependencies and flushes across its three phases accounts for the full ~275 cycles:
 * Phase 1 — Layer 1 table lookups (~85–105 cycles/img, estimate): 192 loads (64 byte + 128 16B paddw loads) and 136 scalar ALU ops have a port floor of ~64–96 cycles (estimate) and a 64-step paddw dependency chain (64 cycles, estimate), plus ~2.1 L1d misses/img from the input stream (~8–10 cycles, estimate).
 * Phase 2 — ReLU + Layer 2 serial addps chain (~65–80 cycles/img, estimate): Layer 2 cannot start until %xmm1 and %xmm0 finish all 64 paddw steps and ReLU (pcmpgtw/pand, 2 cycles, estimate). Then pextrw (3 cyc) \to cwtl (1 cyc) \to cvtsi2ss (4 cyc) \to shufps (1 cyc) \to mulps (4 cyc) takes ~13 cycles (estimate) before the first addps, followed by 12.33 active hidden units accumulating sequentially into the same three registers (%xmm1, %xmm0, %xmm2) at 4 cycles per addps:
   
 * Phase 3 — Unrolled Argmax serial address dependency (~25–35 cycles/img, estimate): Cannot start until %xmm1, %xmm0, %xmm2 complete Layer 2 and store to 0x2f0..0x310(%rsp) (~5 cycles store forwarding, estimate). Then each comiss 0x2f0(%rsp,%rax,4) for c = 2\dots 9 depends on %rax (best), which is conditionally overwritten by mov $c,%eax after jbe whenever a new maximum is encountered (2.16×/img on average).
 * Branch Mispredictions (~50–63 cycles/img, estimate): mlp16.cachegrind-lines.txt records 3.14 mispredicts/img in the hot loop: 1.85/img on if (!h[j]) continue; (177,151 / 95,600) and 1.29/img on the unrolled argmax jbe branches (123,322 / 95,600). At ~16–20 cycles per pipeline flush (estimate), these cost ~50–63 cycles/img (estimate) AND flush the ROB mid-Layer-2 and at the end of argmax, preventing the CPU from overlapping Image i+1's Layer 1 loads with Image i's Layer 2/argmax tail.
   * Why -DBRANCHLESS=1 (_bl1) alone wasn't faster: Removing if (!h[j]) continue; saves ~1.85 mispredicts/img (~30–35 cycles, estimate), but forces execution of the remaining 3.67 zero hidden units/img, adding +14.7 cycles (estimate) to the serial addps critical path (16 \times 4 = 64\text{ cycles} vs. 49.3\text{ cycles}) plus 44 extra instructions/img, while leaving the 1.29 argmax mispredicts/img intact at the end of every image.
4. The Single Best Variant to Settle It: H3 (mlp16_l1only Ablation)
While H9 (branchless argmax on top of -DBRANCHLESS=1) directly attacks the mispredictions, the single variant that best settles the root-cause split across H1–H9 in one run is H3 (mlp16_l1only ablation): timing Layer 1 + ReLU alone while keeping the accumulators observable via a 1-byte store to out[i].
 * Why H3 settles it immediately: mlp16's 607 hot-loop instructions split cleanly into Layer 1 (350 Ir/img, 192 loads, 0 mispredicts) and Layer 2 + argmax (257 Ir/img, 42.5 stack loads, 3.14 mispredicts, 49-cycle addps chain).
   * Outcome A (Predicted by static analysis): mlp16_l1only drops from ~72.7 ns/img (~275 cyc) to ~25–30 ns/img (~95–115 cyc, estimate), achieving ~12–15 instructions/ns. That proves Layer 1 runs near its port floor and that ~160–180 cycles (~43–47 ns/img, estimate) are spent in Layer 2 + argmax (confirming H3 + H9 and ruling out H1/H2 as the primary bottleneck).
   * Outcome B: mlp16_l1only takes >48 ns/img (>180 cyc, estimate). That rules out Layer 2/argmax as the majority cost and proves Layer 1 load/address stalls (H1/H2) dominate.
