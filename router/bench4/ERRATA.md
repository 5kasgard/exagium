# Erratum: Scaffold Effect (arXiv 2607.22585)

Date: 2026-11-18. Ruled by data; owner: decisions follow the data; checked by a separate agent.

This is an erratum filed next to the registered bench-4 plan.

1. **Status.** arXiv 2607.22585 ("Scaffold Effect") is a workshop preprint under
   review at the 5th DL4C Workshop @ ICML 2026. It is **not published**. Any
   earlier reference to it in this bench's plan as "published" is wrong and
   stands corrected here.

2. **Direction.** The paper's direction **differs from bench-4's**. It does not
   test or support bench-4's registered question, so it must not be cited as
   related prior work for bench-4, nor treated as published evidence for it.
