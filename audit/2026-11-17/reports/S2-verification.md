- **A-C1 / A-M2 "live" exposure is overstated.** The runs A cites as live (bench3 on 11-16 and 11-17, bench5 on 11-16) had empty plans (inferred: STATUS says 0 open cells and there is no record commit; job logs were not checked), so the `attempt` job, the only one with the key and the agent container, did not run. Both flaws are real but dormant until someone dispatches or adds cells. Fix before the next bench, not as an emergency.
- **E-01 "High" is too high.** E-03 itself shows nothing consumes the probe. B/C's Medium (red-is-normal noise) fits better.
- **A-M5 "Major" fits a multi-writer repo.** Today only the owner can push, so the real harm is the README:3 wording. Minor, unless outside verifiers are given push access.
- **B-F5 is understated in mechanism, not severity.** In CI, one bad file blocks every proposal, including the ones ahead of it. Medium stands.
- **B-F4 Medium is fair, and the fix is cheaper than B says:** delete `|| true` (keep the `exit $?` echo for the log).
